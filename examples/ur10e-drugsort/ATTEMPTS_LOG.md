# Drug-Sort Pilot Finetuning — Attempts Log & Eval Guide

Backtrace of every pilot finetune/eval attempt in the UR5e→UR10e drug-sort
program, with parameters, results, and diagnoses. Written 2026-07-28 for
external collaborators (companion to `UR10e_DrugSort_Pipeline.ipynb`, which has
the full pipeline, source code, and visuals).

## Which eval to use (the short answer)

**Neither LIBERO nor Isaac Lab.** This dataset is *playground-native*: frames
come from the Lovell AI Robot Playground's Three.js deployment renderer over
MuJoCo-WASM physics, and the earliest, hardest lesson of this program was the
**render gap** — policies score differently the moment the pixels come from a
different renderer. LIBERO has no UR10e embodiment at all, and Isaac Lab has
UR10e assets but not this cell/task — evaluating there would measure the
sim-to-sim gap, not the policy.

The matched eval is the **in-browser closed-loop fold eval** that ships with the
pipeline: `eval_browser_groot.js` driven by `run_vm_eval2.sh`, replaying a
frozen seeded fold of vial poses (n=15; folds 7777 and 8383, generated by
`precompute_plans.py --seed <fold>` — never retuned on). Success = vial lifted
AND seated in the nest pocket (`dxy < 2.5 cm`, upright), scored from physics
ground truth. Metrics per episode: success, lifted, seated, max lift height,
final place distance, gripper state, query count.

Without our serving stack, two useful proxies:
1. **Offline open-loop eval** on held-out episodes of the dataset itself:
   feed `observation.*` at t, compare the predicted 16-step chunk to the
   recorded expert `action[t..t+15]` (per-joint MAE, gripper-transition
   accuracy). Cheap, runs anywhere, and correlates with grasp-phase quality.
2. **The headless expert replayer** (`exec_plans_headless.py`) validates the
   *environment* (scene + plans + physics) on any machine — pin `mujoco==3.10.0`.

We can give you the full closed-loop harness (browser + serving stack) — it
runs on one GPU box; ask Daniel for the branch + RUNBOOK.

## Eval protocol used in every result below

`MODE=bare N=15 MAX_TICKS=1000` (= 125 GR00T queries/ep at 8 action-steps/query),
frozen fold 7777 unless noted, handoff specialist active
(`STEER_HANDOFF=1 ZONE=0.3 MOVECAP=0.8 XY=obs GRASPZ=0.21 VERIFY=4 ATTEMPTS=6
PLACE_Z_HI=0.3 PLACE_TOL=0.03`), observer conditioning served from the
patch-token observer. n=15 ranks ideas; nothing here is an n≥150 claim.

## Common training recipe (all attempts unless noted)

Base `nvidia/GR00T-N1.7-3B`, embodiment tag `NEW_EMBODIMENT`, LR 1e-4, global
batch 16, dataloader workers 8, **ABSOLUTE** joint-position action
representation (relative regressed badly on this cell), 2 camera views
(exterior + wrist, 256×256 @ 20 Hz), 16-step action horizon.
**No photometric augmentation existed before 2026-07-27** (GR00T
`color_jitter_params` defaults to None — only FractionalRandomCrop was active).

## Non-model incidents that corrupted results (check these before blaming a model)

1. **Stale observer weights (cost 2h, 07-27):** serving silently fell back to a
   stock (non-patch) observer → pose estimates degraded to 4–15 cm exactly when
   the arm enters frame. Signature: 0/15, lift 0.0, grip locked 1.00, pad
   2–10 cm, occasional 2–6 m batted vial. Fix: `OBS_WEIGHTS_OVERRIDE` is
   mandatory; verify the conditioner log printed the intended weights loaded.
2. **Env knobs not reaching the service process (block-2):** tuning envs set in
   the launcher never reached the tmux-launched service. `run_vm_eval2.sh` now
   hard-asserts each knob inside `/proc/<svc>/environ` before evaluating.
3. **Environment drift after a reboot:** Chrome/driver updates changed
   execution dynamics enough to matter (part of why 2b's 8/15 is unreproduced);
   local SwiftShader WebGL made rollouts 19 min/ep → all browser stages moved
   to the GPU VM with ANGLE `gl-egl` flags.
4. **MuJoCo version is physics:** 3.11 vs 3.10 flips pocket-well seating.
   Everything is pinned to 3.10.0.

## Distilled lessons

- Train on the deployment renderer's pixels (render gap dominates).
- Capture and transport fail independently; the hybrid handoff
  (specialist captures, pilot transports) beat both pure architectures.
- DAgger inherits the phase distribution of its relabels — balance them.
- Augmentation diversifies appearance only; fix behaviour with data mix.
- Verify the observer and the service env before trusting any eval.
- n=15 folds are for ranking; a success-rate claim needs n≥150 across
  both frozen folds.
